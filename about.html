About Me

The Long
Version

I'm Kenneth — an operator who learned to automate. 10+ years across fintech, BPO, banking, and content marketing taught me how businesses actually run day to day; now I use that operational grounding to build AI automation that solves real problems, not just technical ones. Based in Malaysia, originally from the Philippines.


That grounding started in credit and risk analysis at China Banking Corporation, where I learned how to read data and make decisions under pressure. From there I moved into customer-facing roles at Concentrix as a Sales Advisor, then at TDCX in Kuala Lumpur supporting Stripe merchants, and later freelance Sales and Marketing work at Sharpe Two based in the UAE. Every one of those roles was, in hindsight, training in how operations actually break down — which is exactly what I now design automation to fix.


Today I build AI automation and business analysis projects using n8n and AI tools, and I'm actively looking for remote roles in AI Automation, Business Analysis, or Customer Success. I'm also an active writer, posting regularly on X, Threads, and publishing articles on Substack almost every week.
