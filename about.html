About Me

The Long
Version

I'm Kenneth — based in Klang, Selangor, Malaysia, originally from the Philippines. Over the past 10+ years I've worked across fintech, BPO, banking, and digital marketing in roles that put me at the crossroads of technology and people.


My career started in credit and risk analysis at China Banking Corporation, where I learned how to read data and make decisions under pressure. From there I moved into customer-facing roles — at Concentrix as a Sales Advisor, then at TDCX in Kuala Lumpur supporting Stripe merchants, and later at Sharpe Two in the UAE in sales and marketing.


These days I'm building AI automation personal projects using n8n, and actively looking for remote roles in Customer Success, Business Analysis, or AI Automation. I'm also an active writer — posting regularly on X, Threads, and publishing articles on Substack almost every week.
