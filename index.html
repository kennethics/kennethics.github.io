AI Workflow Automations

n8n & AI tools · Self-initiated
Active

Problem: Repetitive manual work across lead generation, cold outreach, research, email management, and other daily tasks was eating hours that should have gone to higher-value work.

Solution: A set of n8n workflows connected to Claude, Gemini, and OpenAI that handle the repeatable parts of each process end-to-end — from data gathering to first-draft outreach — with a human reviewing before anything goes out. Includes AI agents that browse, extract, and act on web data using Apify.

Outcome: Hours of manual, repetitive work reclaimed every week across five separate workflows — time redirected back into the parts of the job that actually need a person.

Key learning: The highest-value automations aren't the flashiest ones — they're the ones that quietly remove a task someone dreads doing every day.
